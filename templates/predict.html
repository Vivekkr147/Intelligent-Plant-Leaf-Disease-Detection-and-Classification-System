Best Practices for Leaf Photos

For optimal classification accuracy in deep learning systems, input leaf images must satisfy key visual conditions:

  • Single Leaf Focus: Ensure only one leaf occupies the majority of the frame without dense background clutter.
  • Natural & Even Lighting: Avoid harsh sun glare, extreme shadows, or overly dim flash lighting that washes out lesion colors.
  • Sharp & In-Focus: Ensure visual focus is locked onto visible spots, rust pustules, or discolored vein chlorosis.
  • Avoid Blurry / Distant Shots: Photos taken from meters away or with severe motion blur cannot be reliably diagnosed.
