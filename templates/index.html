How PlantCare AI Operates

Designed for simplicity in agricultural environments: upload a clear leaf photograph, verify image integrity, and prepare for instant deep-learning diagnosis.

1
Capture & Upload Leaf

Take a clear photo of an infected or healthy plant leaf and upload it in JPG or PNG format (up to 5 MB) using our responsive drag-and-drop portal.

2
Phase 1 Active
Validation & Preprocessing

Our Python Flask backend and Pillow pipeline inspect the image structure, sanitize filenames, verify color channels, and prepare normalized tensor dimensions.

3
Phase 2 Target
Deep Learning Diagnosis

In Phase 2, our trained MobileNetV2 CNN model will classify the specific disease, calculate confidence, and provide actionable organic treatment advice.

Intelligent Capabilities

Engineered for Accuracy & Usability

PlantCare AI combines modern web engineering with state-of-the-art computer vision to serve farmers with reliable, real-time crop health monitoring.

Early Pathogen Detection

Diagnose fungal spots, bacterial blights, and viral mosaic patterns before physical infection spreads across large crop acreage.

Convolutional Neural Networks

Trained on hierarchical feature extractors that detect microscopic leaf texture variances, chlorosis rings, and vein necrosis.

MobileNetV2 Lightweight AI

Inverted residual blocks and depthwise convolutions ensure lightweight memory usage and sub-second classification speed.

Safe Multi-Tier Validation

Equipped with safe file size limits (5 MB), Pillow image integrity verification, and extension whitelisting to prevent exploits.

100% Mobile Responsive

Fully optimized for handheld smartphones in rural field scenarios as well as high-resolution desktop agronomy labs.

Actionable Organic Advice

Future release pairs each detected leaf condition with organic remedies, chemical recommendations, and prevention practices.
